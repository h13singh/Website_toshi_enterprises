About Toshi Enterprises — OEM Manufacturer

A decade-strong OEM manufacturing partner for businesses across India, specialising in Electrical Accessories and PET Household Products.

Our Story

Manufacturing Excellence from the Heart of Haridwar

Toshi Enterprises was founded with one mission: provide Indian businesses with ISI-certified, factory-direct Electrical Accessories and food-grade PET Household Products at prices that make your margins real.

Our manufacturing unit is located in the Bhadrabad Industrial Area of Haridwar, Uttarakhand — one of India's fastest-growing industrial corridors. From here we serve distributors, wholesalers, retailers and B2B buyers across every state.

We specialise in OEM and private label orders, giving our partners the ability to sell quality products under their own brand without the capital burden of setting up a factory.
