Frequently Asked Questions — Toshi Enterprises, Haridwar

Everything you need to know about ordering, OEM manufacturing, certification and delivery before you reach out.

MOQ varies by product. For most electrical accessories (switches, sockets, junction boxes) MOQ starts at 200–500 pieces. For MCBs it starts at 200 pieces. For PET household products MOQ typically starts at 500–1000 pieces. Contact us with your specific product and quantity — we're flexible for new customers placing first orders.
Yes. We ship from our Haridwar manufacturing unit to all 28 states. We work with reputed logistics partners including Delhivery, BlueDart and Gati. For large orders we arrange direct FTL transport. Delivery typically takes 3–7 business days to most major cities after dispatch.
Yes. We offer comprehensive OEM services — your logo, brand colour, custom label printing and bespoke packaging. Private label is available for both electrical accessories and PET household products. We assist with label design if needed. Minimum run quantities apply. Contact us to discuss your branding requirement.
For new customers: 50% advance + 50% before dispatch. For established accounts with a track record of 3+ orders: credit terms on a case-by-case basis. Payments accepted via NEFT/RTGS/UPI. We issue a formal GST invoice with every order.
For in-stock catalogue products: dispatch within 2–5 business days. For custom OEM or made-to-order products: production lead time is typically 10–21 business days depending on quantity and complexity, followed by dispatch. We provide a confirmed timeline with every order confirmation.
Yes. All our electrical accessories carry mandatory ISI marking as required by BIS. This includes switches, sockets, MCBs and PVC conduit pipes. Our PET household products are manufactured using food-grade material compliant with FSSAI standards. Test certificates are available on request.
Yes. Samples are available for most products. Sample cost and courier charges are borne by the buyer. The sample cost is typically adjusted against the first bulk order. We dispatch samples within 2–3 business days.
